U Copy is a fast, beautiful, and completely private offline clipboard manager and notes app for Android.

Features:
- 100% Offline & Private
- Smart Clipboard Listener
- Markdown Support
- Custom Widgets
- 4 Design Modes
- Import/Export data
- Recycle Bin (Trash) to recover deleted notes
- Quick App Shortcuts for notes and to-dos
- Pin important notes to the top
